Price Guide

What each product really costs per kg, and the price and profit at 15%, 20% and 30% profit margin. Cost = production cost of the stock you hold (kernel, diesel, electricity, losses) + the salaries, rent and running expenses of the days it was produced. For planning only: it sets no prices and changes no figures elsewhere.

Running costs are shared over this many production days a month. Days lost to breakdowns or shortages are not added to the price; they show below as idle-day cost.
